## Honest reading
- The winner was right in every version, but Daryz was the clear pick in all of them (and the favourite), so this is not much of a test.
- The places were mixed: 3-5 of the six finishers were in my top 7. Bay City Roller (2nd, 28/1) was ranked 10th-13th in every version: I rated him 5/10 on fast/good ground because both his Group 1 wins had come on soft and his trainer said he wouldn't run on watered ground; he ran 2nd on good ground, so that ground rating was wrong. Diamond Necklace (3rd) was 7th-9th: stall 16 and the 12f doubt were over-penalised. The models' favourites for the places, Thundering On and Cellini, were NOT in the first six.
- Versions with market prices did not do better than the earlier no-odds versions on this race (the early no-odds v4 had the best average rank, 4.8-5.0). One race cannot validate or invalidate anything.
