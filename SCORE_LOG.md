### What this single race suggests (diagnostic only, n=1)
- Simple beat complex: the plain market, form-only, ground-only and form+distance+ground profiles all had an average rank of about 5.3-5.4 for the placed horses, better than the full model (6.3).
- Removing my DRAW rule helped most (Spearman +0.53 -> +0.65): the rule penalised stalls 15 and 16 that filled 2nd and 3rd.
- Removing the age/sex/trend adjustments barely changed anything (+0.53 -> +0.54, avg rank 6.3 -> 6.1): the 5yo penalty is NOT shown to be harmful or useful by this race. Keep testing.
- Removing all extras (trend, draw, head-to-head) gave +0.67; removing jockey+trainer gave +0.55 (little effect).
- Ratings alone were weak (+0.34), partly because the rating scales were mixed.
- Winner: every profile picked Daryz, so the winner says nothing about which profile is better.
